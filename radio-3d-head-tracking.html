This copy runs on your own computer so it can use your webcam for head tracking. When no face is found, it falls back to the slow drift. Head tracking is right for one viewer: close one eye for the strongest effect. With music playing, bass bands use a 2048-point analysis and treble bands a faster 512-point one, so hits stay snappy. Bars jump up instantly and fall back slowly. The station, song text and logo are placeholders, not the game's. Built with three.js, which can run full-screen on the Pi 5.
